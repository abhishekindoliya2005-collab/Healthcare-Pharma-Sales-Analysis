# Project Report — Healthcare / Pharma Sales Analysis

## Executive Summary
This project analyzes 1,500 synthetic pharmaceutical sales transactions from 2025. The analysis covers products, categories, regions, cities, doctors and sales representatives.

## Overall KPIs
- Total Revenue: ₹6,585,887.69
- Total Profit: ₹792,282.41
- Total Units Sold: 45,146
- Total Orders: 1,500
- Average Order Value: ₹4,390.59
- Overall Profit Margin: 12.03%

## Key Portfolio Findings
- Highest-revenue product: CardioMax 20 (Cardiology) with ₹881,109.51.
- Highest-revenue region: West with ₹1,752,504.54.
- Highest-revenue sales representative: Neha Gupta with ₹960,997.84.
- Highest-revenue month: 2025-10 with ₹654,729.82.

## Business Interpretation
The analysis can be used to identify products and regions that contribute most to revenue, monitor changes in monthly performance, compare sales representatives, and understand where sales resources may need greater attention.

## Limitations
The dataset is synthetic and does not represent real pharmaceutical-company sales, prescriptions, market share, or patient information. Results are intended for analytics practice and interview demonstration.
